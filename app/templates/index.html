What is an AVC?

An Assurance of Voluntary Compliance (AVC) is an agreement used by the Oregon Department of Justice to resolve certain matters without necessarily establishing that the respondent violated the law. And AVC is NOT an admission of guilt.

AVC Explorer organizes information from public records while preserving links to the original source documents.

What is the AVC Explorer for?

The AVC Explorer is an independent research and search tool designed to make Assurances of Voluntary Compliance (AVCs) publicly available through the Oregon Department of Justice easier to find and research.

AVC Explorer does NOT determine whether any person or entity violated the law, and inclusion in this database should not be interpreted as evidence of wrongdoing, liability, or a legal violation. An AVC may resolve disputed allegations and does not necessarily establish that a respondent violated the law.

Information provided by AVC Explorer, including summaries, categories, citations, financial information, and other extracted or indexed data, is provided solely to assist with research and document discovery. It is not legal advice, a legal opinion, or an rely on AVC Explorer as a substitute for reviewing the original document or conducting independent legal research.

AVC Explorer is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Oregon Department of Justice. The original AVC and other official government records are ALWAYS the authoritative sources.
